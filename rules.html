League Rules

  1. Scoring is by twos and threes.
  2. Clear it past the arc. All changes of possession must be taken back beyond the three-point line.
  3. Opening possession. Shoot a three-pointer to start the first game. After that, the losing team from the previous game starts with possession for the new game.
  4. Play all three. All three games are played per match regardless of score.
  5. Final minute. With one minute left on the clock, if the lead is ten points or less, any foul results in a stopped clock and two automatic free throws. If fouled on a made basket, no free throws are awarded. No rebound needed: the opposing team automatically gets possession after both free throws and takes it out on top. The clock restarts when the opposing team inbounds the ball.
  6. Every game counts. The accumulation of points determines the playoff bracket. Wins matter throughout seasonal play.
